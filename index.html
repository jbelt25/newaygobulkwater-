Questions buyers ask us

Where is the station?

Newaygo, Michigan, positioned for operators working Newaygo County, Fremont, White Cloud, Grant, and the surrounding West Michigan area. The exact site address and tanker access details are provided with your reservation confirmation.

Is the water potable?

Tell us your intended use on the LOI form — dust control, hydroseeding, pool filling, agricultural spraying, drinking-water hauling, and other uses have different requirements. We'll confirm in writing, with water quality documentation, that the source fits your use before you sign a purchase agreement.

What does the Letter of Intent commit me to?

Nothing. The LOI is non-binding. It reserves your tier while we confirm details and prepare a written purchase agreement. You only commit when you sign that agreement.

How fast can I actually fill?

The station is designed for 300+ GPM. At 300 GPM, a 3,000-gallon truck fills in about 10 minutes and an 8,000-gallon tanker in about 27 minutes.

What happens after I use up my pre-sale volume?

You can keep filling at standard pay-as-you-go rates, or prepay additional volume. Your account and PIN stay the same.
